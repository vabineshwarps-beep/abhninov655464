# Local SEO Action Plan — Abhinav Loan Associates

Domain: https://abhinavloanassociates.in/
Scope: SEO only. No design, content, form, admin, JavaScript or business-function changes are requested by this plan.

## Primary local search themes
- gold loan in Papanasam
- gold loan in Thanjavur
- loan agency in Thanjavur
- personal loan in Thanjavur
- pawn shop in Thanjavur

## On-page SEO
- One clear H1 per indexable page.
- Unique title and meta description per page.
- Canonical URL on every indexable page.
- Descriptive image ALT text where relevant.
- Contextual internal links between service and location pages.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and duplicated location copy.

## Technical SEO
- Keep robots.txt pointing to the XML sitemap.
- Keep sitemap URLs canonical and indexable only.
- Keep admin/customer pages noindex and excluded from the sitemap.
- Keep the /contact/ canonical destination and the contact.html redirect.
- Preserve existing site functionality and security configuration.

## Local SEO
- Keep the official business name, address and contact details consistent.
- Keep Papanasam and Thanjavur location relevance naturally represented.
- Maintain and optimize the Google Business Profile with the same official business details.
- Build genuine local citations and relevant local links; do not buy or automate spam links.

## Structured data
- Keep accurate first-party LocalBusiness/Service/WebPage/Breadcrumb information where already present.
- Do not add fake review, rating, price or availability schema.

## Competitor-derived priorities
Competitor research indicates that local branch pages commonly expose location, hours, service categories, FAQs/reviews and nearby-branch navigation. Abhinav's implementation should use the useful information architecture patterns while keeping all copy, branding and business facts original.

## Google indexing workflow
1. Deploy the site on https://abhinavloanassociates.in/.
2. Confirm HTTPS, canonical URLs, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
3. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
4. Use URL Inspection for the homepage and key service/location pages.
5. Request indexing for important new/updated pages where appropriate.
6. Monitor Page Indexing, Core Web Vitals and Search Console queries.
